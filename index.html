FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Technical Architecture FAQs

How does Vitals achieve 2,048 Hz raw biopotential capture without conductive skin gel?

Vitals utilizes a proprietary 32-channel dry Ag/AgCl radial electrode array integrated with an ultra-high input impedance front-end (>10 Giga-ohms) and differential Common Mode Rejection Ratio exceeding 142.4 dB. This eliminates the requirement for abrasive skin preparation or conductive electrolytic gels.

How does the automated Blood Pressure Emergency SOS dispatch work?

When the sensor detects a hypertensive crisis (>160 mmHg systolic) or a severe hypotensive step-down drop (<90 mmHg systolic indicative of collapse or syncope), an automated 10-second countdown is armed. If not cancelled by the user, the cellular gateway dispatches high-priority SMS alerts containing the user's exact GPS coordinates and vital metrics to registered family contacts and logs the event to MongoDB.

Is telemetry data stored locally on our premises?

Yes. All telemetry records, device logs, and emergency alert records are stored directly in your local MongoDB Community Edition instance at mongodb://127.0.0.1:27017/vitals_db, ensuring strict HIPAA compliance, data sovereignty, and zero cloud dependency.

What is the end-to-end interface latency?

From raw motor unit action potential (MUAP) biopotential voltage deflection at the wrist to decoded digital actuation, end-to-end processing requires under 3.2 milliseconds, executing entirely on-chip within the Vitals N1 ASIC microcode.
